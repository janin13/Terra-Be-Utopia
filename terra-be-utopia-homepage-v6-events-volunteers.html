About Terra-Be-Utopia

Imagine a place where regeneration becomes something you can walk into.

Where land becomes healthier, food is grown, buildings are created and restored with care, old skills meet new ideas, and people learn by doing.

Terra-Be-Utopia brings these possibilities to life through real-world demonstration projects. Each place can respond to its own landscape, people and needs while connecting land, learning, practical skills, regenerative living and long-term observation.

The aim is not to build one model for everyone. It is to create places where different regenerative approaches can be experienced, questioned, adapted and carried forward.

The first place taking shape

Meet Monte Ventosa.

140 hectares in the Alentejo. Off-grid. Outdoors. A living place taking shape.

Monte Ventosa is the first place where parts of the Terra-Be-Utopia vision are being put into practice: land regeneration, off-grid infrastructure, natural building, food growing, practical learning, courses, volunteering and new ways of bringing useful knowledge into everyday life.

Land can be part of the connection

Some people have land. Others are searching for the right place.

Terra-Be-Utopia can help connect landowners with people looking for land to lease, buy or develop responsibly.

Not every piece of land needs to become a Terra-Be-Utopia demonstration project. Sometimes the most useful role is simply to help the right people find one another.

Become a member

Stay close to the journey — and help make more of it possible.

We are exploring a simple monthly Terra-Be-Utopia membership for people who want to support the work and stay more closely connected.

The exact structure, benefits and contribution level are still being developed. The intention is to keep it accessible, useful and genuinely supportive rather than build another paywall around the vision.

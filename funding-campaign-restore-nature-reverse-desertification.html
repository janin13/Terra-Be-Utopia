The 30-second version

Not another theory. A place where regeneration becomes real.

One person walking a different way may leave barely a trace. When more people begin walking, a path appears. Terra-Be-Utopia is about creating those paths — not by fighting what we no longer want, but by protecting, regenerating and building what we do.

Where

Monte Ventosa

140 hectares in the Baixo Alentejo, Portugal — a Mediterranean landscape already showing the pressure of drought, erosion, heat, wildfire risk and advancing desertification.

What

Restore living systems

Restore water cycles, rebuild soil, strengthen biodiversity, grow food, regenerate ecosystems, protect what remains and create conditions in which life can naturally unfold again.

Why now

A first catalyst

This October, internationally renowned permaculture teacher Geoff Lawton can bring decades of dryland and regenerative landscape knowledge directly onto the land.

The goal

€30,000

To create essential water, sanitation and event infrastructure, prepare the site and help make the first major educational catalyst possible.

What remains

More than a course

The course lasts three days. The infrastructure stays. The knowledge stays. The land continues changing. What we learn can travel far beyond one place.

The bigger vision

Terra-Be-Utopia

Monte Ventosa is intended to become the first living demonstration project of a wider regenerative initiative connecting land, people, knowledge, educators and real-world projects.

Stop feeding what you want to leave behind. Protect what is worth protecting. Regenerate what has been damaged. Create what is missing.

Imagine another path

What if we stopped fighting what we no longer want — and started creating what we do?

The world already contains extraordinary knowledge: permaculture, regenerative agriculture, agroforestry, water retention, natural building, traditional skills, ecological restoration and people who have spent decades learning how living systems recover.

Too often, these people, places and skills remain isolated islands.

Terra-Be-Utopia is about helping those islands find one another — so knowledge becomes practice, practice becomes visible, and visible alternatives become paths that others can walk too.

Another path

What if we stopped feeding what we want to leave behind — and started creating what we want to see exist?

One person walking another way may leave barely a trace. When more people begin walking, a path appears.

We do not all need to follow the same path. We need enough of us to begin creating new ones.

Imagine

Come back in five years. Then ten. Then twenty.

Today, this land is beautiful — and under pressure. Water disappears too quickly. Soil dries. Summers intensify. Desertification is no longer an abstract idea.

Now imagine more water staying in the landscape.
Soil becoming alive again.
Shade returning.
Food growing.
Biodiversity returning.
A landscape becoming cooler, richer and more resilient.

What else could become possible from there?

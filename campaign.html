This is where we begin

There is no polished “after” picture yet.

Monte Ventosa is real land in the Alentejo. Beautiful, imperfect and still missing much of the infrastructure needed to host learning and practical regeneration at scale.

That is not something we want to hide. The first decisions, the setbacks, the things that work and the things that do not — all of it belongs to the demonstration.

The beginning is part of the demonstration.

Imagine if a farm did not have to choose between protecting the land and making a living from it.

Why this matters here

Regeneration has to work for the people who live from the land.

One of the reasons Terra-Be-Utopia matters to us is very practical: farmers and landowners need ways to keep land productive without using it up.

In a dry and fire-prone region, we want to explore and demonstrate approaches that can hold more water in the landscape, rebuild soil, increase fire resilience and keep land economically useful over the long term.

We are not claiming to have all the answers. We want to work first with practitioners who have spent decades doing this, apply the learning on real land, observe what happens and share the results honestly.

Water abundanceDesign to slow, spread, store and cycle water more intelligently.
Fire resilienceExplore safer landscapes without treating repeatedly bare soil as the only possible answer.
Productive landRegeneration that can support livelihoods rather than sit outside them.
Open learningDocument what works, what fails and what has to be adapted locally.

What stays

The course ends. The infrastructure does not.

What is created for the first course becomes the beginning of the next one — and part of the working infrastructure of the demonstration project.

Water systemPump, storage and distribution remain useful on the land.
Community kitchenA practical base for courses, volunteers and future builds.
ToolsEquipment that keeps working after the event is over.
Classroom materialsTarps, anchoring and reusable materials are kept and repurposed.
KnowledgePractical experience can be tested, refined and shared.
RelationshipsParticipants can become collaborators, partners, teachers and future hosts.
